# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19 + TypeScript + Vite, Polpo UI library, Tailwind CSS v4, pnpm 11 and Node 24. Design system, mockups and user flows in Figma (Juan's Figma, Pro plan); the site implements direction D · Cuarto oscuro.

## Users

- Brands and companies looking for product, fashion and campaign photography.
- Private individuals booking portraits or personal sessions.
- Editorial and tourism clients (magazines, hotels, nature/landscape tourism).
Architecture firms and real-estate were not selected as a primary audience, even though architecture is a core style of her work.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and services platform for photographer Lina María Nieto Gómez, from Bogotá, Colombia. It exhibits her work and her services so prospective clients can judge her eye and contact her to hire her. Success: a visitor sees work in their category and starts a quote request or a WhatsApp conversation.

## Positioning

A photographer whose own eye comes from nature, landscape and architecture, applied with the same composure to people, fashion and product work.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Instagram, referrals or search, usually on a phone; brands and editorial clients often review on desktop before contacting. Contact happens two ways: a quote form (service, date, project description) and a WhatsApp button with a prefilled message. Lina answers with a proposal.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Portfolio by category: nature, landscape, architecture, people, fashion, product.
- Services presentation; contact through quote form + WhatsApp.
- Language: Spanish (Colombia) primary. Bilingual not decided.
- Prices: not decided; no prices shown unless Lina provides them.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Lina María Nieto Gómez.
- Colors she likes (binding): blue, sober tones of green, occasional gold details.
- Reference board on Pinterest (co.pinterest.com/linamanigo18/portfolio); screenshots pending from the user.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real photos yet: mockups use generated reference images, labeled provisional, to replace with Lina's work.
- No testimonials, clients, awards, press or prices on hand; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The photograph leads; the interface recedes.
2. Every category a client might hire her for is two taps from the start.
3. Contacting her is always one step away and never pushy.
4. Honest content only: real work, real services, no invented proof.
